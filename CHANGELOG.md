## 0.1.0

* Initial release.
* `HintedImagePicker`: single-image picker with hint/thumbnail states, InkWell ripple, hover elevation, lightened hint overlay with customizable icon, camera/gallery source selector.
* `HintedMultiImagePicker`: multi-image picker with `+N` badge showing additional selected image count.
* Cross-platform image rendering via `Image.memory` (no `dart:io` dependency).
